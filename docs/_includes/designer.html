Letter size on your clock

The grid shows the letters at this size, in the font of the front plate.

  • Type . for a minute dot.
  • Type an apostrophe right after a letter to put both in one square, like the O' in O'CLOCK.
  • Squares you don't need for the time can hold anything: a name, a message, random letters or a symbol from the row above.

3. Choose the words

The letter for the day of the week and the four minute dots are optional: leave them empty if your grid doesn't have them. If you use them, fill in all seven days or all four dots.

A special word is a name or message hidden among the letters, which lights up all day on its dates with a slow pulse, such as a birthday or anniversary. Click its letters and type up to four dates as day-month, like 14-03.

A fun phrase is shown instead of the time on the days and between the times you choose, like IT IS BEER O'CLOCK on Friday from 17:00 to 18:00. Click all its letters, including IT IS and O'CLOCK if you want them.

For each phrase in the list, click the letters the clock lights. Only the minutes and hours are needed; the opening words and the hour words for the full hour are optional.

4. Check your clock

Try a few times and days and read what the clock says. You can also save a picture of the clock at that time.

5. Order your clock

This is your front plate: 30 × 30 cm, with the letters at the size you chose. The black border of about 2.5 cm around the letters is part of the design.

Use the details from your order. Fill in the same name and email address you used when ordering your clock, and its order number. If the order number doesn't match an order, your clock won't go into production. All three fields are required. See our privacy statement for what we do with them.

Ordered more than one clock? Send a design for each clock, numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on. If several clocks get the same design, send it once and say in your email how many clocks it is for.
