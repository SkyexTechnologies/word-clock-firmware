# Privacy statement

This statement explains which personal data Skyex Technologies receives when
you design a letter grid, order a word clock or use this website, and what we
do with it.

Questions about your data: [skyex.technologies@gmail.com](mailto:skyex.technologies@gmail.com).

## When you send us a design

In step 5 of the [designer](design-your-own.html) you fill in your name, email
address and order number. Nothing is sent to us at that moment: the designer
runs in your browser. We only receive these details when you email us your
design.

| What we receive | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name, email address and order number | To match your design to your order and to contact you about it | Performing our agreement with you (your order) |
| Your design file (the letters and which squares light up) | To make the front plate and set up your clock | Performing our agreement with you |
| The email itself | To handle your order and answer your questions | Performing our agreement with you |

We keep your name, email address and design for 2 years after your clock is
delivered: the warranty period, so we can help you if your clock needs repair.
Order records (such as invoices) are kept for 7 years, as Dutch tax rules
require.

We receive email through Gmail (Google). We don't share your details with anyone
else and don't use them for marketing.

## What stays in your browser

While you work on a design, the designer keeps it in your browser's local
storage, including the name, email address and order number you filled in, so
your work isn't lost when you reload the page. This data stays on your device
and is never sent to us or anyone else. To remove it, start again with an empty
grid in step 1, or clear this website's data in your browser.

The designer doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server,
GitHub's servers process your IP address to deliver the pages, and may keep it
in their logs for security. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The website loads everything, including its fonts, from GitHub Pages itself and
contains no third-party trackers.

## Your word clock

The clock doesn't send us any personal data and has no account or tracking. To
work, it contacts:

- **GitHub Pages**, every 6 hours, to check for firmware updates. GitHub sees
  the IP address of your home network.
- **Internet time servers** (pool.ntp.org), to get the time when Home Assistant
  doesn't provide it.
- **Home Assistant** on your own network, if you connect it.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we have about you,
or to send it to you. Email
[skyex.technologies@gmail.com](mailto:skyex.technologies@gmail.com). We may
need to keep order records for as long as the law requires. If you're not
satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data
Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
